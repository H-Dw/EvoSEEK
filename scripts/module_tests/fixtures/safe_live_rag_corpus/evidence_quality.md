# Evidence-quality constraints

Retrieval similarity is a discovery signal, not scientific credibility,
task applicability, or decision authority. A retrieved passage may inform a
hypothesis, but it must remain context-only unless an independent evidence gate
establishes its provenance and permitted decision use.
