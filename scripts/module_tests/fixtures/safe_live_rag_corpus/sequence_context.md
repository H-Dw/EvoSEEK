# Sequence-context constraints

The effect of a substitution can depend on the surrounding sequence context.
Single-change evidence therefore does not establish the direction or magnitude
of a combined-change effect. Benchmark feedback should update a hypothesis only
after the proposed combination is evaluated by the benchmark oracle.
