# Decision log

## DL-001 — Preserve the existing knowledge base

The bundle is written to `gb1_directive_v1` and will receive a separate immutable index. Existing corpora and overlays are not overwritten.

## DL-002 — Convert evidence into directives

Generic prose such as “epistasis is background dependent” does not tell the Scientist what to compare. Each included claim therefore has to produce a trigger, class-level action, matched comparison, boundary, falsifier, and abstention rule.

## DL-003 — Keep stability and binding separate

Independent thermodynamic measurements support a folding/stability gate. They do not directly identify higher binding fitness. Cards label this as a constraint or confidence modifier, never as a binding optimum.

## DL-004 — Use contact geometry without benchmark labels

An in-memory calculation from the official 1PGA coordinate file found minimum heavy-atom distances of 3.81 Å for positions 39/54 and 3.67 Å for positions 41/54. This supports paired reasoning about local packing. No benchmark variant or fitness record was queried.

## DL-005 — Quarantine discovery overlap

Three discovery results matched excluded target-label or dataset-overlap categories. They were not opened. Only opaque exclusion events were retained; their content did not influence cards or subsequent queries.

## DL-006 — Research-candidate authority only

The bundle is model-authored and has no human co-signature. It is eligible only for the controlled paired benchmark, with runtime permission `explanation_only`; it is not a production release.
